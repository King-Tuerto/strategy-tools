How the QSPM works:
The Quantitative Strategic Planning Matrix is the most analytical tool in the strategy formulation framework. You will enter key internal and external factors (from your IFE/EFE analyses) along with their weights. Then, for each factor, you assign an Attractiveness Score (1-4) for each strategy. The tool calculates a Total Attractiveness Score (TAS) for each strategy — the highest TAS indicates the best strategic alternative.

How to use this tool:
• Factor Name: Key internal/external factors from your IFE and EFE analyses.
• Weight: 0.00 to 1.00 — internal weights must sum to 1.00, external weights must sum to 1.00 (two separate pools).
• AS (Attractiveness Score): 1 = Not attractive, 2 = Somewhat attractive, 3 = Reasonably attractive, 4 = Highly attractive. Leave blank if the factor does not affect the strategy choice.
• WAS (Weighted Attractiveness Score): Calculated automatically as Weight × AS.
