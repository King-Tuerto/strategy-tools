How the IE Matrix works:
The IE Matrix plots your company using two scores: the IFE Total Weighted Score (X axis) and the EFE Total Weighted Score (Y axis). Both scores range from 1.0 to 4.0. The matrix is divided into 9 cells grouped into three strategic zones:
• Zone 1 (Grow & Build) — Cells I, II, IV
• Zone 2 (Hold & Maintain) — Cells III, V, VII
• Zone 3 (Harvest & Divest) — Cells VI, VIII, IX

📘 How to use this tool:
• Enter the Total Weighted Score from your IFE and EFE matrices. Scores range from 1.0 to 4.0.
• Optionally, add multiple divisions if analyzing a multi-division company. Each division gets its own IFE and EFE score and is plotted separately.
• The matrix determines which strategic zone applies: Grow & Build, Hold & Maintain, or Harvest & Divest.
