How the Grand Strategy Matrix works:
The Grand Strategy Matrix positions a company in one of four quadrants based on two assessments: Competitive Position (strong vs. weak) and Market Growth Rate (rapid vs. slow). Each factor is scored from -3 to +3. The resulting quadrant indicates which broad strategic direction is most appropriate:
• Quadrant I (top-right) — Rapid Growth + Strong Position
• Quadrant II (top-left) — Rapid Growth + Weak Position
• Quadrant III (bottom-left) — Slow Growth + Weak Position
• Quadrant IV (bottom-right) — Slow Growth + Strong Position

📘 How to use this tool:
• Use the sliders to assess your company's Competitive Position (weak to strong) and Market Growth Rate (declining to rapid growth).
• Each score ranges from -3 (very weak / rapid decline) to +3 (very strong / rapid growth).
• Base your assessment on your prior analyses (IFE, EFE, CPM) and industry research.
• The tool will plot the intersection point on the Grand Strategy Matrix and recommend appropriate strategies.
