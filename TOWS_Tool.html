About the TOWS Matrix:
The TOWS Matrix, developed by Weihrich (1982), is a structured extension of the SWOT framework specifically designed for strategy generation. While SWOT identifies factors, TOWS cross-references them to produce four categories of strategic alternatives:

• S-O Strategies (Maxi-Maxi) — Use strengths to exploit opportunities (most aggressive growth strategies)
• W-O Strategies (Mini-Maxi) — Overcome weaknesses by leveraging opportunities (turnaround strategies)
• S-T Strategies (Maxi-Mini) — Deploy strengths to neutralize or avoid threats (competitive defense strategies)
• W-T Strategies (Mini-Mini) — Minimize weaknesses and avoid threats (retrenchment or survival strategies)

In the results matrix, your specific factors appear in the row and column headers so each strategy cell explicitly references which factor combinations it addresses.

📘 How to use this tool:
• Strengths (S): Internal advantages your organization possesses over competitors (distinctive capabilities, proprietary assets, superior resources).
• Weaknesses (W): Internal limitations or deficiencies that place the organization at a competitive disadvantage.
• Opportunities (O): External trends or conditions the organization could exploit for competitive advantage (market growth, technology shifts, regulatory change).
• Threats (T): External forces that could negatively affect performance (competitive moves, economic downturns, disruptive technology).
• Enter at least 1 factor per section. You may add up to 10 factors per section.
• Your factors will appear in the TOWS Matrix headers on the next screen so you can cross-reference them when writing strategies.
