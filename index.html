Your work stays on your computer. Nothing you type is sent to this website or to your instructor. The Strategy Toolkit remembers your work in the browser you used, on that computer only. The individual tools do not save: finish in one sitting and use Print / Save PDF before you close the page.

Downloading. Download puts the tool in your Downloads folder. Double-click the file to open it; it works without the internet. A downloaded copy does not see work you did on this website. In the Toolkit, use Save to file and Open file to move a project between them.
