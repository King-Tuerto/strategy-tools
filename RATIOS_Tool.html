How financial ratio analysis works:
Ratios turn raw financial statements into comparable measures of performance. This tool calculates fourteen ratios in four groups:
• Liquidity — can the firm pay its short-term bills? (current, quick)
• Leverage — how much of the firm is financed by debt? (debt-to-assets, long-term debt-to-equity, times interest earned)
• Activity — how efficiently does the firm use its assets? (inventory, fixed asset, total asset, and receivables turnover; collection period)
• Profitability — how much does the firm earn? (gross margin, operating margin, ROA, ROE)

You will enter the two most recent years of financial data, then (optionally) your assumptions for three projected years. The projected ratios show whether your recommended strategy is financially realistic.

📘 How to use this tool:
• Enter the oldest year in the first column and the newest year in the second.
• Use one unit throughout (for example, all figures in millions). Do not type commas or dollar signs.
• Enter interest income, tax credits, and treasury stock as negative numbers.
• Shaded rows calculate automatically. The balance check must show that assets equal liabilities plus equity.
• Sources: the company’s 10-K (Form 10-K annual report), or a financial site such as Yahoo Finance or Morningstar.
  Use the example to see how the tool works, then replace it with your company’s numbers.

📘 Building the projections:
• Work in this order: income statement first, then the balance sheet from the bottom up — dividends and equity, then liabilities, then assets.
• Percent rows are entered as percents (type 5 for 5%). Dollar rows marked net new are the change for that year only; the tool adds it to the prior year.
• The “Newest year” column shows the historical figure for reference. Do not blindly copy it; adjust it to reflect your recommended strategy.
• Cash is the plug figure. It is calculated so the balance sheet balances. If projected cash is negative or unrealistically high, revisit your assumptions.
  A starting point only; change the numbers to match your strategy.
