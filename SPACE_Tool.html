How the SPACE Matrix works:
The SPACE Matrix evaluates a company across four dimensions to determine its strategic posture: Financial Strength (FS) and Industry Strength (IS) are scored on a positive scale (+1 to +6), while Competitive Advantage (CA) and Environmental Stability (ES) use a negative scale (-1 to -6). The dimension averages are combined to produce an (X, Y) coordinate plotted on a four-quadrant matrix:
• Aggressive (top-right) — strong internal and external position
• Competitive (bottom-right) — strong industry in an unstable environment
• Defensive (bottom-left) — weak position across dimensions
• Conservative (top-left) — financially strong but competitively weak

📘 How to use this tool:
• Financial Strength (FS) and Industry Strength (IS): Score each factor from +1 (worst) to +6 (best).
• Competitive Advantage (CA) and Environmental Stability (ES): Score each factor from -1 (best) to -6 (worst).
• Click any factor name to customize it. Use + Add Factor to add rows (max 10) or × to remove them (min 1).
• The average for each dimension updates automatically as you enter scores.
