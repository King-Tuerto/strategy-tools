What is a Perceptual Map?
A perceptual map plots how customers perceive competing brands along two strategic dimensions that you choose (for example, Price vs. Quality, or Innovation vs. Tradition). Each brand is placed on a coordinate grid from -5 to +5 on each axis. The map reveals positioning gaps, crowded competitive spaces, and opportunities for differentiation. In the next step you will define your axes and plot your company alongside competitors.

How to use this tool:
• Part A: Name each axis and its endpoint labels. Choose two attributes that meaningfully differentiate brands in your industry.
• Part B: Position each brand on a scale from -5 to +5 for each axis. Negative values correspond to the left/bottom label; positive values correspond to the right/top label. Zero is neutral/center.
• Your company is pre-filled as the first row and cannot be removed. Add at least 2 competitors for a meaningful comparison.
