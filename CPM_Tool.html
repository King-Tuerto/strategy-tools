How the CPM works:
You will identify Critical Success Factors (CSFs) for the industry, assign each a weight (0.00–1.00, summing to 1.00), then rate each company on every CSF from 1 (major weakness) to 4 (major strength). The total weighted score reveals each company's overall competitive position.

Critical Success Factors

Weight CSFs and rate each company

📘 How to use this tool:
• Weight each CSF from 0.00 to 1.00 based on importance to industry success. All weights must sum to 1.00.
• Rate each company per CSF: 4 = major strength, 3 = minor strength, 2 = minor weakness, 1 = major weakness.
• Weighted Score = Weight × Rating. The total for each company indicates its competitive position.
• You can edit CSF names, add rows (up to 10), or remove rows.
