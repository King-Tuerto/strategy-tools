How the BCG Matrix works:
You will enter your company's business units or product lines along with their Relative Market Share and Industry Growth Rate (%). Each unit is plotted on a 2×2 matrix:
• Stars ⭐ — high share + high growth (invest to grow)
• Question Marks ❓ — low share + high growth (invest or divest)
• Cash Cows 🐄 — high share + low growth (harvest cash)
• Dogs 🐕 — low share + low growth (divest or reposition)

📘 How to use this tool:
• Relative Market Share = your unit's market share divided by the largest competitor's share. A value > 1.0 means you lead the market.
• Market Growth Rate (%) = the industry growth rate for that unit's market. Can be negative for declining markets.
• Revenue ($M) is optional — if provided, it sizes the bubble on the chart. Leave blank for equal-sized bubbles.
• Adjust the threshold dividers below to customize where the quadrant lines are drawn. Defaults: share = 1.0, growth = 10%.
> this value = High share (left side). Default: 1.0
> this value = High growth (top half). Default: 10%
