How the IFE Matrix works:
You will identify key strengths and weaknesses for this company, then assign each a weight (0.00 to 1.00) reflecting its importance. All weights across both sections must sum to 1.00. Each factor also receives a rating: strengths are rated 3 (minor) or 4 (major), weaknesses are rated 1 (major) or 2 (minor). The total weighted score ranges from 1.0 to 4.0, with 2.5 as the midpoint.
